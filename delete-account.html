Delete Your Account

Last updated: September 2026

You have the right to delete your ExamSimplify account and all associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to submit a deletion request and what happens to your data.

How to Request Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through either of the following options:

1

Email Us

Send an email to support@examsimplify.com with the subject line Account Deletion Request and include your registered email address in the message body.

Use the button below to send a pre-filled request:

Delete Account Request
2

Contact Us In-App

Open the ExamSimplify app, tap the support icon and send us a message requesting account deletion. Include your registered email address so we can locate and remove your account.

What Data Is Deleted

Upon account deletion, the following personal data is permanently removed:

  • Your full name, email address and profile information
  • All exam results and score history
  • Bookmarked and saved questions
  • Subscription and payment records (transaction references)
  • Device information and login history
  • Push notification tokens
  • All other personally identifiable information associated with your account

What Data Is Retained

After deletion, we retain the following for a limited period:

  • Anonymised usage statistics — aggregated, non-identifiable data retained for up to 90 days for internal analytics and legal compliance. This data cannot be linked back to you.

Deletion Timeline

Your account deletion request will be processed within 30 days of receipt. You will receive a confirmation email once your account and data have been permanently deleted.

Things to Know Before You Delete

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once deleted, your exam history, progress and saved questions cannot be recovered.

  • Active subscriptions are not automatically cancelled — please cancel your subscription through Google Play before requesting deletion to avoid future charges
  • If you have a pending subscription refund, please resolve this before submitting a deletion request
  • You can create a new account at any time after deletion

Contact Us

If you have any questions about account deletion or your personal data, please contact us:

  • Email: support@examsimplify.com
  • Privacy policy: examsimplify.com/privacy
